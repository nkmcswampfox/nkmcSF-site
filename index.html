The History of the Nam Knights of America MC

The Nam Knights of America Motorcycle Club (MC) was founded in July 1989 in Carlstadt, New Jersey, by a small group of Harley-Davidson riding combat veterans.

The Origin

A unique brotherhood of Vietnam combat veterans and law enforcement officers.

Growth into an International Organization

The club now spans over 80 chapters across the United States and Canada.

DISCLAIMER: The Nam Knights of America MC does not claim any territory as evidenced by their “America” bottom rocker worn by members across the U.S.
